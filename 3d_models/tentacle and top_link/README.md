#  Tentacle Design

##  Purpose
The tentacle functions as the **end-effector**, designed specifically for **soft gripping of objects**.

---

##  Material
- **PLA (Final choice)**  
- Selected for structural stability and manufacturability  

---

##  Iterations
5–6 design iterations were developed, exploring:
- Material choices (PLA, TPU, Resin)  
- Number of segments  
- Interlocking/locking mechanisms  
- Tolerances and fit  

---

##  Actuation
- Operates using a **separate tendon system**  
- Independently controlled from continuum links  

---

## 🧩 Design Features
- Modular segmented structure  
- Interlocking joints for controlled bending  
- Designed for adaptive gripping  

---

## 📈 Performance
- Achieves reliable gripping for moderately sized objects  
- Demonstrates adaptive contact due to compliant structure  

---

## ⚠️ Challenges
- Tolerance sensitivity between segments  
- Trade-off between flexibility and strength  

---

All design versions and final assembly images are included.
