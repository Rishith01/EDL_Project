# Enclosure Design

## Purpose
The enclosure serves as the **outer housing** of the system, providing structural support and protection for internal components. It also guides the robot through a constrained exit point.

---

## Manufacturing
- **Material**: 6mm Acrylic  
- **Process**: Laser cutting  
- **File Format**: DXF  

---

## Design Features
- Panel-based design for easy assembly  
- Central opening for robot deployment  
- Slots and edges designed for alignment  
- Internal space for housing electronics and structure  

---

## Assembly
- Panels are assembled using **interlocking wedge-based joints**  
- Additional **tape reinforcement** is used for structural safety  
- Screws used where necessary for mounting
